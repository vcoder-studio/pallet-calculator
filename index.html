How to calculate how many boxes fit on a pallet

You can do it by hand in four steps. The calculator above does exactly this.

  1. Boxes per layer (Ti). Divide the pallet length by the box length and the pallet width by the box width. Round both down and multiply. Then turn the box 90° and repeat; keep the larger number.
  2. Number of layers (Hi). Subtract the pallet height from the max height allowed, divide by the box height and round down.
  3. Total boxes. Multiply Ti × Hi.
  4. Check the weight. Multiply the total by the box weight. If it is above the max load weight, the weight limit decides: divide the max load weight by the box weight and round down.

Example

A 12 × 10 × 8 in box on a 48 × 40 in pallet (5.5 in tall) with a 96 in height limit: 48 ÷ 12 = 4 and 40 ÷ 10 = 4, so 16 boxes per layer. (96 − 5.5) ÷ 8 = 11.3, so 11 layers. Total: 16 × 11 = 176 boxes, load height 93.5 in. If each box weighs 15 lb and the pallet may carry 2,000 lb, the weight limit allows 133 boxes (9 layers, the top one partial).

Why rotate some boxes?

When the box does not divide the pallet evenly, turning part of the layer 90° can fill the leftover strip. This is called a mixed (or block) pattern. The calculator above shows how many extra boxes per layer a mixed pattern would add; the PRO extension draws the pattern.

FAQ

How do I calculate how many boxes fit on a pallet?

Boxes per layer × number of layers. Boxes per layer = (pallet length ÷ box length, rounded down) × (pallet width ÷ box width, rounded down), checking both box orientations. Layers = (max height − pallet height) ÷ box height, rounded down.

What does Ti × Hi mean?

Ti is the number of boxes in one layer (tier); Hi is how many layers high they are stacked. Ti × Hi is the total number of boxes on the pallet.

Does the max height include the pallet?

Yes, in this calculator. Enter the pallet height separately: it is subtracted before layers are counted, and the load height result includes the pallet.

What if my boxes are heavy?

Enter the box weight and the max load weight. The calculator then caps the total at what the weight limit allows and tells you how many would fit by height alone.
